01What we cover

The work around the work.

Your technicians and quality team do the calibration. We take on the administration, follow-up, and coordination that slow it down.

Technician capacity
Strip the data entry, status calls, and re-keying out of the technician's day so more time goes to the bench.
Recall follow-up and revenue
Track calibration due dates and follow up with customers on schedule, with your team approving what goes out.
Quoting
Faster, more consistent quotes from your own pricing rules, and follow-up on the ones that go quiet.
Turnaround
Clear status from receipt to return, so instruments do not sit waiting for a handoff nobody noticed.
QA and certificate administration
Organize the paperwork around QA review and certificate delivery. Approval decisions stay with your qualified people.
Work-to-cash
Shorten the gap between the certificate going out and the invoice being paid.
Owner visibility
Current numbers on backlog, turnaround, recall capture, and receivables, without a spreadsheet scramble.
02Where we draw the line

Support for the operation. Not a substitute for judgment.

Automation handles the repetitive administration around calibration work. Measurement decisions, technical judgment, and required QA approvals stay with your qualified people.

  1. Measurement and technical judgment stay human

    Automation never decides a measurement, tolerance, or method.

  2. QA approval stays with your approvers

    Required reviews and sign-offs are performed by your qualified staff.

  3. A person approves customer communication

    Recall notices, quotes, and updates are reviewed before they go out.

  4. Your existing systems stay

    We connect and improve the workflow around your lab software. No rip and replace.
