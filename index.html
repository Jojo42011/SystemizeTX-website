01 The Problem

It all runs
through you.

Most Texas operators do not have a technology problem. They have a systems problem.

  • 01

    Every answer lives in your head

    Pricing, scheduling, who to call, what to chase. The whole company waits on one person: you.

  • 02

    Fifteen tools and none of them talk

    CRM, spreadsheets, whiteboards, group texts. The truth of the business is scattered everywhere and lives nowhere.

  • 03

    Cash lags weeks behind the work

    The job is done but the invoice is not out. Receivables drift while everyone is busy on the next job.

  • 04

    Growth just adds chaos

    Every new crew and every new contract means more office work, more phone calls, more late nights holding it together.

It works, until it stops working. And it will never scale past your attention. That is the problem we exist to remove.
